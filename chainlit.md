# DE-Rigor-Agent 🧬

An AI assistant for **bulk RNA-seq differential expression analysis**. It checks
your data, evaluates suitable statistical methods, and runs the analysis with
an explanation of the results.

📁 **Upload** your expression data and sample metadata.

💬 **Describe** the comparison you want, such as “Compare disease versus control, adjusting for batch.”

🔎 **Review** the proposed method, answer any questions, and explore the results.

⬇ **Download** the conversation as JSON and HTML using the Download command.
